Godot "Dodge the Creeps" demo. Everything that isn't ours keeps its
original license:

- **Game code** — from the official
  [Godot demo projects](https://github.com/godotengine/godot-demo-projects),
  MIT © Godot Engine contributors ([LICENSE](LICENSE)).
- **Art & audio** (`art/`) — distributed with the official demo and carried
  over unchanged; see the upstream
  [demo repository](https://github.com/godotengine/godot-demo-projects)
  for per-asset attribution.
- **Font** — Xolonium (`fonts/`), SIL Open Font License 1.1; full license
  text and history in [`fonts/LICENSE.txt`](fonts/LICENSE.txt) and
  [`fonts/FONTLOG.txt`](fonts/FONTLOG.txt).
- **CheddaBoards addon** (`addons/cheddaboards/`) — MIT © CheddaTech Ltd,
  license included in the addon folder.
